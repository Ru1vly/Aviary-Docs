# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 2026

Aviary is an open-source website auditing engine and documentation platform. We prioritize transparency, security, and minimal data collection.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

Aviary runs audits locally on your machine or inside your CI/CD pipelines:
- We do not transmit audited page contents to external servers.
- We do not track personal identifying information (PII).
- We do not sell, rent, or monetize your auditing data or traffic.

## 2. Server Logs and Hosting

When visiting the Aviary documentation site (`aviary-docs.vercel.app`), standard anonymous HTTP access logs (such as IP address, user agent, and timestamp) are processed by Vercel infrastructure strictly to serve requests, prevent abuse, and ensure DDoS mitigation.

## 3. External Links

Our documentation links to third-party repositories and resources (such as GitHub). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or contents of third-party websites.

## 4. Contact and Questions

If you have questions about privacy or wish to report a security vulnerability, please open an issue on the [Aviary GitHub repository](https://github.com/Ru1vly/Aviary/issues).
